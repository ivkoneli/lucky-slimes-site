Delete your Lucky Slimes account

How to permanently delete your account and data · Lucky Slimes by IVKOGAMES

Option 1: From inside the game (fastest)

  1. Open Lucky Slimes.
  2. Tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm twice.

You need an internet connection. Deletion happens immediately and the game restarts as a brand-new player.

Option 2: Without the game installed

Send an email to ivkoneli.ai@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion" and include one of:

  • Your Player ID, shown at the bottom of the game's Settings screen, or
  • The Google Play Games gamer name you linked to the game, or
  • The Google Play order number (starts with GPA.) of any purchase you made in the game, which you can find in your Google Play purchase history.

We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your player account (player ID and any linked Google Play Games sign-in).
  • Your cloud save: all progress, currencies, slimes, upgrades and settings.
  • The purchase records stored with your save.
  • On the device you delete from: everything the game stored locally.
  • Your analytics identifier is reset, so new activity can't be linked back to the deleted account.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Payment records held by Google Play or the Apple App Store. These belong to the store and are kept under their policies. They are also what lets you restore one-time purchases on a new account.
  • Aggregated analytics, which no longer identifies you, may be kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months before it is deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports may be kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Diamonds and other currency you bought and haven't spent are lost. One-time purchases (packs, upgrades) can be restored afterwards from Settings ▸ My Purchases using the same Google Play account.
