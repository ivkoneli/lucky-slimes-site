Privacy Policy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE] · Applies to the Lucky Slimes mobile game

Lucky Slimes ("the game", "we", "us") is made by [DEVELOPER NAME]. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

The short version: we don't ask for your name, email or phone number. The game creates an anonymous player ID so your progress can be saved in the cloud. We use Google services to store saves, measure how the game is played, process purchases and show optional reward ads. You can delete your account and data at any time from inside the game.

1. Information we collect

Information created when you play

  • Player ID. A random identifier created automatically the first time you open the game (via Firebase Authentication). It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Game progress. Your save data: stages, currencies, slimes, upgrades, settings and similar game state. We also store the app version, platform (Android/iOS) and the time of the last save.
  • Purchase records. When you buy something, we store which item you bought, the store's order ID and the time, so we can deliver it and restore it later. We never see or store your card or payment details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

Information from an account you choose to link

  • If you choose Save your progress and sign in with Google Play Games, we receive your Play Games player identifier and gamer name so your save can follow you to a new device. We do not receive your Google password or email address.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and analytics data (Google Analytics for Firebase): in-game events such as reaching a stage, opening the shop or completing the tutorial, plus device model, operating system, app version, language, approximate country/region (derived from IP address) and an app-instance identifier.
  • Crash and diagnostic data (Firebase Crashlytics): if the game crashes, a report with the stack trace, device model, OS version and app state at the time.
  • Advertising data (Google AdMob): only when you choose to watch an optional reward video. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to serve and measure ads, and, where you have allowed it, to personalise them.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data.

2. How we use it

  • To run the game and save and restore your progress across sessions and devices.
  • To deliver, restore and verify purchases, and to help with purchase problems or refunds.
  • To show optional reward ads and give you the reward.
  • To understand how the game is played, fix bugs and crashes, and improve balance.
  • To prevent fraud and cheating involving purchases.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for anything outside the game.

3. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We process data to perform our contract with you (providing the game and your purchases), for our legitimate interests (keeping the game working, secure and improving it), and with your consent where the law requires it, for example for personalised advertising. In those regions the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time (see section 7).

5. International transfers

Our service providers may store and process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. They use safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep it

  • Save data and purchase records: for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your cloud save and player ID are removed immediately.
  • Analytics data: kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: kept by Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Records we must keep by law (for example tax records of purchases) are kept for as long as the law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the game, open Settings ▸ Delete Account. If you no longer have the game installed, see Delete your account.
  • Ads: reward ads are always optional. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. On Android, go to Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads; on iOS, go to Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking.
  • Access, correction, portability, objection: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access the data we hold about you, correct it, receive a copy, or object to or restrict its processing. Email us with your Player ID (shown at the bottom of the game's Settings screen) and we will respond within 30 days.
  • You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Lucky Slimes is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Your data is stored with Google Firebase, which encrypts data in transit and at rest. Security rules mean each player's save can only be read and written by that player's own account. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
